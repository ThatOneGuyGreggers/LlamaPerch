# Welcome to LlamaPerch 🦙

A little menu bar llama for your local llama.cpp server.

- [Changelog](https://github.com/ThatOneGuyGreggers/LlamaPerch/wiki/Changelog): everything built, reviewed, fixed, and renamed so far.
- [Setup and troubleshooting](https://github.com/ThatOneGuyGreggers/LlamaPerch/wiki/Setup): get a model running and solve common problems.
- [Validation](https://github.com/ThatOneGuyGreggers/LlamaPerch/wiki/Validation): test coverage, the Intel acceptance machine, real-model smoke checks, and remaining work.
- [Development releases](https://github.com/ThatOneGuyGreggers/LlamaPerch/releases): downloadable Intel macOS builds and checksums.

Start with **General → choose llama-server**, then **Models → add a GGUF**, save, and start. Your model files stay in their original folders.
